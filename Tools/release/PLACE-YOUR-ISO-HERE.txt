Put your own Shadow of the Colossus ISO in this folder, then run Play.bat.

Supported disc: Europe/Australia PAL, SCES-53326, version 1.00.
Required size: 4,635,918,336 bytes. Any filename ending in .iso is fine.

You can also keep the ISO elsewhere and drag it onto Play.bat.
No compilation or developer tools are needed for this prebuilt release.
